05 Capital Markets · Academic Academic

Capital Markets · Jindal Worldwide Ltd.

Corporate Actions — Financial Impact

What was the financial impact of the 4:1 bonus issue on shareholder wealth, capital structure, EPS and market valuation?

Approach Bonus mechanics — 4:1 ratio, record date 28 February 2025 — followed through share capital transformation, reserve capitalisation and EPS measurement against market price movement.

Loan Moratorium Impact Analysis

How do moratorium structures function in Indian banking, and what are their effects on borrowers and banks?

Approach Secondary research across RBI circulars, banking reports and the COVID-19 moratorium case study, with interest and EMI illustrations.
Key finding A moratorium suspends EMI timing, not the obligation — interest accrues and total repayment rises while borrowers avoid defaulter classification.

Equity Valuation · Financial Modelling

Bata India — Store Renovation Capex & Margin Modelling

Can Bata India’s shift toward casual footwear, store renovations and store-network optimisation produce enough growth, margin improvement and free cash flow to support its market value?

Approach Store-driven revenue build, product-mix and operating-leverage analysis, renovation and new-store capex, FCFF modelling, DCF scenarios and peer cross-check, with explicit Ind AS 116 lease treatment.
