Capital markets · Jindal Worldwide

Corporate Actions — Financial Impact

How does a bonus issue on a listed company move entitlements and per-share metrics?

ApproachThe corporate action was traced through announcement, ex-date mechanics and entitlement calculation, then measured on per-share figures.

Finding4:1 bonus issue (record date 28 February 2025) capitalised ₹80.21 Cr of reserves into share capital (₹20.05 Cr → ₹100.26 Cr); EPS declined 84.8% (₹0.92 → ₹0.14) with BVPS down ~78%. Academic finding — not investment advice.
