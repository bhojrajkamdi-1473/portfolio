05 Capital Markets · Academic Academic

Capital Markets · Jindal Worldwide Ltd.

Corporate Actions — Financial Impact

What was the financial impact of the 4:1 bonus issue on shareholder wealth, capital structure, EPS and market valuation?

Approach Bonus mechanics — 4:1 ratio, record date 28 February 2025 — followed through share capital transformation, reserve capitalisation and EPS measurement against market price movement.
Key finding Share capital moved from ₹20.05 Cr to ₹100.26 Cr on reserve capitalisation of ₹80.21 Cr; EPS adjusted from ₹0.92 to ₹0.14 following the 4:1 issuance, with BVPS declining ~78%.
Scope & limitation Single-company study; bonus effects not isolated from broader market movement; no peer bonus-issue comparison. Academic project — not investment advice.
Why it matters — demonstrates the ability to translate a corporate action into changes in capital structure and per-share financial metrics.
