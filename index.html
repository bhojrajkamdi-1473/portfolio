05 Capital Markets · Academic Academic

Capital Markets · Jindal Worldwide Ltd.

Corporate Actions — Financial Impact

What was the financial impact of the 4:1 bonus issue on shareholder wealth, capital structure, EPS and market valuation?

Approach Bonus mechanics — 4:1 ratio, record date 28 February 2025 — followed through share capital transformation, reserve capitalisation and EPS measurement against market price movement.
Key finding Share capital moved from ₹20.05 Cr to ₹100.26 Cr on reserve capitalisation of ₹80.21 Cr; EPS adjusted from ₹0.92 to ₹0.14 following the 4:1 issuance, with BVPS declining ~78%.
Scope & limitation Single-company study; bonus effects not isolated from broader market movement; no peer bonus-issue comparison. Academic project — not investment advice.
Why it matters — demonstrates the ability to translate a corporate action into changes in capital structure and per-share financial metrics.

How much does position sizing, rather than stock selection alone, determine risk-adjusted portfolio outcomes across divergent investor risk profiles?

11 Valuation · Financial Modelling

Equity Valuation · Financial Modelling

Bata India — Store Renovation Capex & Margin Modelling

Can Bata India’s shift toward casual footwear, store renovations and store-network optimisation produce enough growth, margin improvement and free cash flow to support its market value?

Approach Store-driven revenue build, product-mix and operating-leverage analysis, renovation and new-store capex, FCFF modelling, DCF scenarios and peer cross-check, with explicit Ind AS 116 lease treatment.
Model conclusion The report’s base DCF estimates ₹420 per share against its stated ₹613 reference market price dated 1 October 2026; bear and bull scenarios are ₹124 and ₹851.
Limitation Limited direct access to primary filings; renovation economics and category mix are not separately disclosed by Bata. Terminal value contributes about 80% of enterprise value in the base case, so the valuation is highly sensitive to discount rate and terminal growth.
