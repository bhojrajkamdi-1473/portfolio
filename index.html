05 / Approach

How I approach financial work.

A practical working approach — not an official framework, standard or methodology. Each step is demonstrated on real work below.

01Understand 02Analyse 03Verify 04Interpret 05Document 06Conclude
01 — Understand: define the business, transaction or financial question. 02 — Analyse: examine statements, transactions, ratios, assumptions or supporting information relevant to the question. 03 — Verify: trace important conclusions back to source data, documents or calculations. 04 — Interpret: connect the evidence to the financial or business issue. 05 — Document: present the analysis, assumptions, findings and limitations. 06 — Conclude: state what the evidence supports — and what it does not.
Audit — Transaction verification

Question → trace the transaction → inspect supporting evidence → verify the accounting treatment → document the conclusion. Applied across 3,000+ vouched transactions and 1,000+ scrutinized ledgers at G M Achhra & Associates.

Corporate action — Jindal 4:1 bonus

Question → analyse reserve capitalisation → examine the share-count effect → evaluate EPS and BVPS changes → interpret shareholder impact. EPS declined 84.8% (₹0.92 → ₹0.14); BVPS declined ~78%, per the report's analysis.

Financial analysis — Tech Mahindra

Question → examine ten years of statements → analyse ratios and trends → build two-year projections → state what the trends support. Liquidity held above 1:1 throughout; gross margin 60–63%; operating margin troughed at 4.40% in 2023–24.
